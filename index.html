About

I am a quality engineering leader specializing in mobile monetization platforms at enterprise scale. At Digital Turbine — a publicly traded company (NASDAQ: APPS) whose platform reaches over 800 million devices worldwide — I architect and execute the quality strategy that safeguards a $341.6M revenue pipeline spanning device activation, firmware delivery, carrier integration, and advertiser attribution.

My work sits at the intersection of quality assurance, AI/ML engineering, and platform reliability. I have designed and validated over 1,080 test scenarios across the company's most revenue-critical subsystems, built AI-powered tools that transform how the entire QA organization operates, and created enterprise platforms that integrate six major systems into a unified quality workflow. These are original engineering contributions that operate at a scale affecting hundreds of millions of devices globally.

Beyond testing, I have driven the adoption of AI in quality assurance processes — creating TestForge, a production-deployed platform on Google Cloud Run that uses Vertex AI and Claude to generate test scenarios from product requirements, and DTLogScout, an AI chatbot that analyzes Android device logs using Claude's tool_use capability grounded in source code analysis. These tools represent a new paradigm in quality engineering — one that I pioneered within Digital Turbine.

Leadership & Industry Contributions

Driving innovation, expanding platform capabilities, and shaping quality engineering practices

Innovation

Pioneered AI-Driven Quality Assurance

Led the adoption of artificial intelligence in quality assurance processes at Digital Turbine. Created TestForge — the company's first AI-powered test generation platform — deploying it to production on Google Cloud Run with Vertex AI and Claude integration. This established a new paradigm where test scenarios are generated from product requirements using large language models, fundamentally transforming the QA workflow from manual authoring to intelligent automation.

Leadership

Unified Enterprise Quality Platform

Designed and built a unified platform connecting six enterprise systems — Confluence, Jira, GitLab, TestRail, Slack, and Vertex AI — into a single quality workflow. This cross-platform integration enables requirements traceability from product documentation through test case generation to execution results, with full audit logging and team notifications. The platform serves the entire QA organization, not a single team.

Impact

Expanded Advertiser Measurement Ecosystem

Designed the attribution integration for two new Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP) integrations — Branch and Kochava — expanding Digital Turbine's supported measurement ecosystem from 3 to 5 partners. This directly improved advertiser experience by enabling brands to use their preferred measurement platform while receiving accurate attribution data from the 800M+ device network. Direct brand revenue grew from 22% to 47% of total brand revenue during this period.

Impact

Coverage spans the complete monetization lifecycle — from device eligibility (FOTA/OOBE) through app delivery (Verizon MCS/Drawbridge) to attribution reporting across all 5 MMP partners — ensuring every install is tracked, verified, and correctly attributed.

Contributions to the Mobile Industry

Advancing the quality and reliability of mobile app delivery at a global scale

End-to-End Mobile Monetization Quality

Established a comprehensive quality framework covering the entire mobile app monetization lifecycle — from the moment a device is activated (OOBE), through firmware-over-the-air updates (FOTA), carrier-specific app installations, to advertiser attribution reporting. My validation work ensures that every app delivered to 800M+ devices is correctly targeted, installed without corruption, and accurately attributed to the right advertising source. In an industry where a single undetected bug can silently affect millions of devices and cause significant revenue loss, this end-to-end quality coverage is critical infrastructure.

Advancing Attribution Accuracy Across the Ecosystem

Mobile attribution — the process of connecting an app install to the ad that drove it — is the backbone of the $300B+ digital advertising industry. I designed and validated the quality frameworks for 5 major Mobile Measurement Partners (AppsFlyer, Singular, Adjust, Branch, Kochava), including building the Branch and Kochava integrations from scratch. My work covers click-through, clickless, and view-through attribution paths, AES-GCM encrypted data flows, retry logic for transient failures, and referrer broadcasting accuracy. This ensures advertisers across the ecosystem receive reliable, trustworthy install data.

Raising the Bar for Multi-Carrier Delivery Reliability

Mobile carriers depend on reliable, secure app delivery to their subscribers. I designed carrier-specific validation frameworks — including Verizon's MCS/Drawbridge integration with JWT authentication, SHA-256 hash verification, and install status broadcasting — that ensure every carrier partner receives verifiable proof that apps were delivered correctly. My test coverage spans 37+ carriers and 12+ OEM manufacturers globally, addressing the combinatorial complexity that makes mobile delivery one of the hardest quality problems in the industry.

Pioneering AI in Mobile QA

Traditional mobile QA relies on manual test case authoring — a process that struggles to keep pace with rapid feature development and the combinatorial explosion of device/carrier/OEM configurations. I changed this by creating AI-powered quality tools that are now used in production: TestForge generates test scenarios from product requirements using Vertex AI and Claude, while DTLogScout diagnoses device issues through natural language conversations grounded in source code. These tools represent a shift in how the mobile industry can approach quality at scale — moving from reactive, manual testing to proactive, AI-assisted quality assurance.

Protecting Revenue Integrity in Mobile Monetization

In on-device mobile monetization, every failed attribution means lost revenue and every incorrect eligibility decision means wasted advertiser spend. I built the quality safety net that prevents these failures — 1,080 test scenarios covering every edge case from concurrent attribution for the same package, referrer decoding with invalid UTF-8, FOTA delivery to devices with identical fingerprints, to JWT token expiry in carrier integrations. Each scenario represents a potential production incident that, left uncaught, would silently degrade the reliability that advertisers and carriers depend on.
